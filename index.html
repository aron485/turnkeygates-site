Answers Before You Build

Frequently Asked Questions

I’m thinking about adding a new gate. What types of gates do you install?
We install a variety of gates to suit your needs and style. Popular options include swing gates in wood, iron, or vinyl, and space-saving automatic slide gates ideal for driveways.
How can I improve the security of my entry points?
We offer security door repair and installation services. We can address hinge issues, replace locks, and fix broken door closers to ensure your entryways are secure.
Do you offer any preventative maintenance services?
Yes, we do! Regular maintenance keeps your gates and doors functioning smoothly and prevents costly repairs down the road. We offer preventative maintenance plans for your peace of mind.
My automatic gate opener isn’t working properly. Do you repair openers?
Yes! We can diagnose and repair existing openers or install a brand-new one. We also program remotes for easy access.
My garage door spring broke. Can you replace it?
Of course! Broken springs are a common issue, and our technicians are experts at replacing them safely and efficiently. We can also fix other garage door problems like opener malfunctions or damaged panels.
How can I get a free quote for gate or door service?
Simply contact TurnKey Gates! We offer free consultations and quotes. We’re happy to discuss your specific needs and recommend the best solution for your property.
