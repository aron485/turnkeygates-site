About Slidell, LA

Slidell, pronounced as /slaɪˈdɛl/, is situated on the northeastern edge of Lake Pontchartrain within St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana, USA. As per the 2020 census, its population stood at 28,781. It forms an integral part of the New Orleans−Metairie−Kenner metropolitan statistical area.

Interesting or Fun Facts about Slidell, LA

Slidell was established on the northern shore of Lake Pontchartrain in 1882 and 1883 while the New Orleans and Northeastern Railroad (N.O.N.E.) was being built. This railroad line linked New Orleans with Meridian, Mississippi.
