All About Mandeville, LA

About Mandeville, LA

Mandeville, situated in St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana, USA, had a population of 11,560 according to the 2010 U.S. census, which grew to 13,192 by the 2020 census. Positioned on Lake Pontchartrain’s north shore, just south of Interstate 12, Mandeville faces New Orleans and its southern suburbs across the lake. It forms part of the New Orleans–Metairie–Kenner metropolitan area.

Things To Do in Mandeville, LA

Mandeville boasts a variety of parks including Paul Cordez Park, Lakefront Gazebo, Sunset Point, and Tyler Thomas Park. Sunset Point Fishing Pier and Park stretches more than 400 feet (120 m) out into Lake Pontchartrain.
