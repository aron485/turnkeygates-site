All About Madisonville, LA

About Madisonville, LA

About 40 miles north of Lake Pontchartrain is Madisonville, which is situated in relation to New Orleans. The town is conveniently reachable from both the north and the south via I-12, LA Highway 21 South, LA Highway 22, and the Tchefuncte River. The town may be reached from the south via the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway.

Interesting or Fun Facts about Madisonville, LA

Originally inhabited by Native Americans and later colonized by the French, this city was ultimately named in honor of President James Madison. Its rich heritage is evident in thriving antiques and arts markets, as well as the Madisonville Museum, located within the historic circa 1911 courthouse.

Public Transportation in Madisonville, LA

Madisonville, LA, nestled in Tangipahoa Parish, Louisiana, offers a serene lifestyle for those seeking a slower pace. Though transportation choices are limited in this small city, they do exist. The primary public transportation option is the Tangipahoa Parish Transit System (TPT), serving various destinations within Madisonville and nearby areas. Private taxi services are also accessible at reasonable rates. Moreover, bike lanes are available, providing residents the flexibility to travel by bicycle. These transportation amenities ensure convenient mobility for Madisonville residents within the city and its vicinity.
