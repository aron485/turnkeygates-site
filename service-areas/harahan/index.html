All About Harahan, LA

About Harahan, LA

Harahan is a small city located in Jefferson Parish, Louisiana. Harahan was originally known for being a small farming community as well as a railroad and industrial town, but it is now mostly residential.

Although the city is very close to New Orleans, it distances itself from the hustle and bustle with its peaceful nature.

Things to Do
  • Taste delicious Cajun and Creole cuisine at popular restaurants.
  • Explore the Mississippi River Trail by boating, biking, or taking a stroll.
  • Play golf with friends and family at the Colonial Golf and Country Club.
Notable Residents
  • Mike Yenni, Jefferson Parish president
  • Tracy Porter, NFL player 
  • Louise Hynes, a costume designer known for her work on TV shows
