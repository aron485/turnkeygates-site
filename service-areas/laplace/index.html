All About Laplace, LA

About Laplace

Laplace, Louisiana, nestled along the Mississippi River in southeast Louisiana, transcends its reputation as a mere gateway.  This vibrant city boasts a unique identity that blends small-town charm with cultural richness and exciting possibilities.

Laplace offers a unique experience that goes beyond serving as a gateway.  It’s a city steeped in culture, brimming with outdoor adventures, and rich with history.  Whether you’re a family seeking fun, a foodie on a culinary quest, or a history buff yearning for a glimpse into the past, Laplace offers a welcoming atmosphere and a chance to create lasting memories.

Things To Do
  • Festival Fun: Immerse yourself in the lively spirit of Cajun Country!  Join the annual crawfish boil, a true Louisiana tradition, or explore nearby towns known for their vibrant Cajun festivals celebrating music, food, and dance.
  • Explore Cajun Culture: Delve deeper into the rich Cajun heritage by visiting nearby towns or exploring local museums and cultural centers. Learn about the unique traditions, crafts, and cuisine that define this fascinating region.
  • Mississippi Magic: The mighty Mississippi River is a playground for outdoor enthusiasts. Take a scenic boat tour and discover the wonders of the swamp ecosystem, teeming with wildlife and unique plant life.
  • Cast a Line: Test your fishing skills and enjoy the serenity of the river.  Laplace offers opportunities to catch local fish species, making it a perfect activity for nature lovers and anglers alike.
Notable Residents
  • Edward “Kid” Ory: a trombonist and bandleader, was a pioneer of New Orleans jazz and a formative influence on Louis Armstrong.
  • Wellman Braud: a pioneering jazz bassist who played with some of the biggest names in early jazz, was also born in LaPlace.
  • Jared Butler: is a basketball player who currently plays for the Orlando Magic after a standout college career at Baylor University.
  • A. J. Duhe: a former linebacker who played for the Miami Dolphins after attending Louisiana State University.
Nearby Suburbs
  • Destrehan, Louisiana: Located just north of LaPlace, Destrehan is a small town with a historic district.
  • Mount Airy, Louisiana: Another small town north of LaPlace, Mount Airy offers a more rural setting.
  • Luling, Louisiana: East of LaPlace, Luling is a growing community with a mix of residential and commercial development.
  • Boutte, Louisiana: Located southwest of LaPlace, Boutte is a small town that’s known for its alligator farms.
Interesting / Fun Facts
  • Early Jazz Haven: LaPlace played a vital role in the development of early jazz. Pioneering jazz figures like trombonist and bandleader Edward “Kid” Ory hailed from LaPlace. Ory even mentored the legendary Louis Armstrong!
  • Sugar Rush: Sugar plantations were the lifeblood of LaPlace for much of its history. These grand estates not only shaped the local economy but also left behind a legacy of beautiful historic architecture.
  • Swamp Stars: Just southwest of LaPlace, you’ll find Boutte, Louisiana, famous for its alligator farms. LaPlace boasts easy access to these fascinating creatures, offering a unique tourist experience.
  • Holiday Cheer: LaPlace isn’t far from Destrehan, Louisiana, known for its annual Destrehan Plantation Christmas Festival. This festive event offers a glimpse into the opulent holiday traditions of the antebellum South.
Public Transportation
  • River Parishes Transit Authority (RPTA): This is the main public transportation provider in LaPlace.
  • Paratransit services: For seniors and individuals with disabilities, there are paratransit services available through the St. John Council on Aging (COA) and the St. John Arc.
  • Rideshare Apps: Major rideshare companies like Uber or Lyft operates in LaPlace.
  • Taxis: taxi services is available in Laplace.
