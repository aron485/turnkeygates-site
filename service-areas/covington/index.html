About Covington, LA

Covington is a city located in St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana, United States. It serves as the parish seat.

According to the World Population Review, as of 2024, the population of Covington is 11,722. The city is situated at the confluence of the Bogue Falaya and Tchefuncte River. Covington is a part of the New Orleans–Metairie–Kenner metropolitan statistical area.

Things To Do
  • Shop in the Covington Market Square, a well-liked hub for community activities in the city center.
  • Start your hike or bike ride on the Covington trail at the 31-mile paved Tammany Trace rails-to-trails.
  • Visit the Insta Gator Ranch to see alligators in the pristine lake in a climate-controlled setting.
Notable Residents
  • Walker Percy, author and essayist
  • Harry Reeks, landscape painter and combat artist for the U.S. Marine Corps.
  • Leon Rene, songwriter
Interesting / Fun Facts
  • The world’s largest bronze statue of former president Ronald Reagan is found in Covington.
  • The town’s original name was “Wharton” after its founder John Wharton Collins. It was later renamed Covington in honor of a War of 1812 general.
  • Covington has been a filming location for several movies. “Dead Man Walking” (1995), “Eve’s Bayou” (1997), and “Beautiful Creatures” (2013) were all filmed at least partially in the city.
Public Transportation

COAST and St. Tammany Parish Government jointly operate STAR Transit, which offers rural and urban transportation services. The transportation is based on a demand response program and is subject to availability.
