The Need for Gates

When it comes to ensuring safety and security, having a gate is essential. Whether it’s for your residential property or a commercial establishment, installing a gate offers numerous benefits that you shouldn’t overlook. Here are some compelling reasons why you need a gate for your Gretna property.

1. Enhanced Security

One of the primary reasons for having a gate is to enhance security. A gate acts as a physical barrier, preventing unauthorized access to your property. It serves as a deterrent for potential criminals and intruders, reducing the risk of theft, vandalism, and other security breaches. With a gate in place, you can have peace of mind knowing that your property is well-protected.

2. Controlled Access

A gate allows you to have control over who enters your property. With the use of access control systems such as keypads, intercoms, or card readers, you can restrict entry to only authorized individuals. This is especially beneficial for gated communities, as it ensures that only residents and their guests can enter. Controlled access also provides added privacy, keeping unwanted solicitors away.

3. Increased Property Value

Installing a gate can significantly increase the value of your property. Potential buyers and renters are often attracted to properties with added security features like gates. It adds a sense of exclusivity and prestige, making your property more appealing in the competitive real estate market. So, if you’re considering selling or renting out your property in the future, having a gate can be a wise investment.

4. Safety for Children and Pets

If you have children or pets, a gate can provide a safe and secure environment for them to play freely. It acts as a physical barrier, preventing them from wandering off or accessing dangerous areas such as roads or swimming pools. With a gate, you can have peace of mind knowing that your loved ones are protected within the confines of your property.

5. Aesthetically Pleasing

Gates come in various designs, materials, and styles, allowing you to choose one that complements your property’s aesthetic. Whether you prefer a modern, sleek gate or a more traditional and ornate one, you can find options that enhance the overall appearance of your property. A well-designed gate can add curb appeal and make a lasting impression on visitors.

6. Traffic Control

In commercial establishments, a gate can help manage the flow of traffic and ensure orderly entry and exit. This is particularly useful for businesses with limited parking spaces or those that require controlled access for security reasons. By implementing a gate system, you can prevent congestion and maintain a smooth traffic flow, enhancing the overall efficiency of your operations.

Types of Gates We Offer

Gates not only provide a physical barrier, but they also act as an entry point control mechanism. At TurnKey Gates, we offer a wide range of gate options to suit your specific needs. Let’s take a closer look at the types of gates we offer:

1. Access Gates

Access gates are designed to control the entry and exit of vehicles and people. These gates can be operated manually or automatically, depending on your preference. Access gates are commonly used in residential communities, commercial properties, and industrial facilities.

2. Electric Gates

Electric gates, also known as automated gates, are becoming increasingly popular due to their convenience and enhanced security features. These gates can be operated remotely, either by using a keypad, key fob, or even through smartphone applications. Electric gates are ideal for homeowners who prioritize convenience and want to add a touch of luxury to their property.

3. Single Gates

Single gates are the most common type of gate used in residential properties. As the name suggests, these gates consist of a single panel that swings or slides open to allow entry. Single gates are easy to install and are a cost-effective solution for homeowners who want to secure their property while maintaining a stylish aesthetic.

4. Sliding Gates

Sliding gates are a popular choice for properties with limited space or steep driveways. These gates slide horizontally to open and close, making them an excellent option for areas where a swinging gate might not be practical. Sliding gates provide a sleek and modern look while maximizing the available space.

5. Swing Gates

Swing gates are a classic choice that adds elegance and charm to any property. These gates open and close by swinging either inward or outward, similar to a door. Swing gates are available in various designs, materials, and sizes, allowing homeowners to choose the style that best fits their taste and property.

Factors to Consider When Selecting a Gate

  • The purpose of the gate: Consider whether the gate is for security, privacy, or simply aesthetic purposes.
  • Material and durability: Assess the different materials available for gates and choose one that is durable, weather-resistant, and suits the style of your property.
  • Size and height: Determine the appropriate size and height of the gate based on your needs, such as accommodating vehicles or providing privacy.
  • Budget: Consider the cost of the gate, including installation and maintenance, and choose one that fits within your budget while still meeting your requirements.

About Gretna, LA

Situated across the Mississippi River from the bustling city of New Orleans is Gretna. More than just New Orleans’ neighbor, Gretna boasts a rich history, a charming small-town feel, and unique cultural experiences.

As of 2022, Gretna’s population stands at approximately 17,200 people.

Things To Do

  • Explore Gretna’s Historic District: Immerse yourself in 19th-century architecture with a stroll through the National Register of Historic Places district. Admire the unique buildings and landmarks like the David Crockett Firehouse, the oldest volunteer fire company in the nation.

  • Step Back in Time: Visit the Gretna Green Blacksmith Shop and witness traditional blacksmithing techniques. Railroad enthusiasts can explore the Southern Pacific Freight Depot and the Illinois Central Caboose museum.

  • Embrace German-American Heritage: Learn about the influence of German immigrants on Louisiana at the German-American Cultural Center.

Notable Residents

  • Marlon Dominique Favorite is a former American football defensive tackle.

  • Claiborne Joseph Cheramie, better known by his stage name Joe Clay, was an American rockabilly musician.

  • Emmett Louis Hardy was an American jazz cornet player during the early 1900s.

Nearby Suburbs

  • Algiers: Located directly across the Mississippi River from Gretna, Algiers offers a vibrant cultural scene and stunning river views.
  • Harahan: Known for its peaceful atmosphere and scenic lakes, Harahan provides a relaxing contrast to the bustling city life.
  • Terrytown: A family-friendly suburb bordering Gretna, Terrytown offers a variety of restaurants and shops.

Fun Facts about Gretna

The famous spice-maker Zatarain’s was founded in Gretna in 1889.

Gretna was incorporated in 1913, absorbing the section of McDonogh within the Jefferson Parish boundaries.

The ferry system played a significant role in Gretna’s growth.

Public Transportation

JeT is a bus service provider that operates in Jefferson Parish, including Gretna. They offer different types of fare options such as single rides, day passes, and monthly passes.

The Regional Ride Pass is also available which allows for unlimited rides on JeT and RTA buses and streetcars for 24 hours after the first use.

The W6 Gretna Local route connects the historic downtown of Gretna to the Algiers Ferry Terminal via Wilty Terminal, which also connects to other routes.

Additionally, taxis, ride-sharing services, and car rentals are available for transportation. If you’re looking for a scenic river crossing between Gretna and New Orleans, the Algiers Ferry is also an option.
