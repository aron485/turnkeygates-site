All About Kenner, LA

About Kenner
  • Kenner, Louisiana is a suburban city located in Jefferson Parish, just minutes from New Orleans.
  • If you’ve ever flown into New Orleans, you’ve been to Kenner, as it’s home to the Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport.
  • Kenner is the largest incorporated suburban city of New Orleans, with a population of around 66,448 people.
Things To Do
  • Explore Rivertown: This 16-block historic district along the Mississippi River is a great place to soak up Kenner’s history.
  • Learn about astronomy at the  Louis J. Roussel Jr. Planetarium & MegaDome Cinema: This planetarium offers educational shows about space and the cosmos, while the MegaDome Cinema showcases movies and documentaries on a giant dome screen.
  • Take a trip back in time at the  Louisiana Toy Train Museum: This museum houses a large collection of antique model trains, including Lionel trains, O gauge trains, and HO scale trains. Kids will love seeing these classic toys up close.
  • Spend a day at the Pontchartrain Center: This convention center complex hosts a variety of events throughout the year, from trade shows and concerts to sporting events and family-friendly festivals.
Nearby Suburbs
  • Metairie, LA: This vibrant city is known for its upscale shopping malls, like Lakeside Shopping Center, numerous restaurants serving cuisine from around the world, and exciting nightlife.
  • Harahan, LA: A quieter community compared to Metairie, Harahan offers a suburban feel with a mix of residential neighborhoods and commercial areas.
  • Gretna, LA: Located directly across the Mississippi River from New Orleans, Gretna boasts a rich history and cultural heritage.
  • River Ridge, LA: Known for its family-friendly atmosphere, River Ridge offers a peaceful suburban environment with excellent schools.
Notable Residents
  • Chris Howard: This former NFL player spent his college career at LSU before being drafted by the Jacksonville Jaguars.
  • Jon Batiste: This Grammy Award-winning musician, singer, and bandleader even has a song titled “I’m from Kenner” highlighting his roots in the city.
  • Edmond J. Muniz: This former mayor of Kenner and captain of the Krewe of Endymion played a significant role in the city’s history.
  • Lloyd Price: This iconic R&B vocalist, known as “Mr. Personality,” was another resident of Kenner.
Interesting or Fun Facts
  • Plane Crash and Pan Am 759 Memorial: In 1982, Pan Am Flight 759 tragically crashed in a Kenner neighborhood due to a microburst. While a devastating event, a memorial now stands to honor the victims
  • Movie Magic: Kenner in Pop Culture: Believe it or not, Kenner has made it onto the silver screen a couple of times. The 1989 movie “Parenthood” featured scenes filmed in the Kenner City Park, and the 2006 film “Déjà Vu” used the Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport for some shots
  • Kenner’s First High School Still Stands: Founded in 1923, Alfred Bonnabel High School is the oldest public high school in Kenner and is still in operation today.
  • Kenner’s Carnival Claim to Fame: Kenner is home to the Krewe of Endymion, one of the largest and most popular Mardi Gras parades known for its extravagant floats and celebrity guests.
Public Transportation
  • Jefferson Parish Transit (JP Transit): JP Transit operates a network of buses throughout Kenner.
  • Ridesharing Apps: Uber is a popular ridesharing app that operates in Kenner.
  • Taxis as an alternative to Ridesharing: While Uber might be the more popular option, taxis are still available in Kenner.
