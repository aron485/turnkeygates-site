Why Should You Install Gates for Your Home in Hammond?

When it comes to home security and curb appeal, installing gates for your home is a wise decision.

If you’re still on the fence about installing gates, here are some compelling reasons to consider:

  1. Enhanced Security

    One of the primary reasons to install gates is to enhance the security of your home. Gates act as a physical barrier, deterring potential intruders and making it harder for unauthorized individuals to gain access to your property. With the right gate system, you can control who enters your premises, ensuring the safety and privacy of your family.

  2. Improved Privacy

    Privacy is a top priority for many homeowners, and gates offer a simple yet effective solution. By installing gates, you can create a boundary between your property and the outside world, preventing unwanted visitors or prying eyes from seeing into your home. This allows you to enjoy your personal space without any disturbances.

  3. Curb Appeal

    Gates add a touch of elegance and sophistication to any property. They come in various designs, materials, and styles, allowing you to choose one that complements the architectural aesthetics of your home. A well-designed gate can significantly enhance the curb appeal of your property, making it more attractive to potential buyers if you ever decide to sell.

  4. Increased Property Value

    In addition to improving the aesthetics, gates can increase the value of your home. Potential buyers are often willing to pay more for homes with added security features, such as gates. Installing gates is an investment that not only provides immediate benefits but also adds long-term value to your property.

  5. Better Control of Access

    With gates, you have complete control over who enters your property. Whether it’s through a keypad, intercom, or remote control, you can easily grant or deny access to visitors. This level of control is especially important for homes with children or pets, as it ensures their safety by preventing them from wandering off or encountering strangers.

  6. Reduced Traffic Noise

    If you live in a busy area or near a main road, gates can act as a barrier against traffic noise, creating a quieter and more peaceful environment within your property. This can significantly improve the quality of life for you and your family, allowing you to enjoy outdoor activities without the constant disturbance of traffic sounds.

Types of Gates We Offer in Hammond

If you’re looking to enhance the security and accessibility of your property, then TurnKey Gates has got you covered.

Let’s take a closer look at the types of gates we offer:

Access Gates

Access gates are designed to control and manage the entry and exit of vehicles and individuals to your property. These gates provide a high level of security and privacy, ensuring that only authorized personnel can gain access.

Access gates are commonly used in residential communities, commercial buildings, and industrial estates. At TurnKey Gates, we offer access gates in various designs, materials, and sizes to suit your specific requirements.

Electric Gates

Electric gates provide convenience and added security to your property. With the ability to be operated remotely, these gates make it easy for you to control access without having to manually open and close them.

Electric gates are ideal for both residential and commercial properties, offering a seamless entry and exit experience.

At TurnKey Gates we offer a wide range of electric gates that are durable, reliable, and easy to use.

Single Gates

If you’re looking for a gate that is simple yet effective, then our single gates are the perfect choice. Single gates are designed to accommodate a single entry or exit point, making them suitable for smaller properties or areas with limited space. These gates can be customized to match your property’s aesthetic and security requirements.

Sliding Gates

Sliding gates are a popular choice when it comes to larger properties or areas with limited space for swing gates. These gates operate horizontally, sliding open and close along a track.

Sliding gates are known for their smooth and quiet operation, making them ideal for residential driveways, commercial properties, and industrial complexes. At TurnKey Gates, we offer sliding gates that are not only functional but also aesthetically pleasing.

Swing Gates

Swing gates are a classic and elegant choice for properties that have ample space for their operation. These gates swing open and close on hinges, creating a grand entrance and adding a touch of sophistication to your property.

Swing gates are available in single or double configurations, providing options for different property sizes and designs.

Factors to Consider When Selecting a Gate

  • The type of gate suitable for your property, such as a swing gate, sliding gate, or overhead gate, should be considered based on the available space and layout of your property.
  • The level of security and privacy required should be taken into account when selecting a gate. Some gates are designed with additional features like keypad entry systems or video surveillance options.
  • The material of the gate is an important factor to consider. Options include wood, wrought iron, aluminum, or steel, each with its own benefits in terms of durability, maintenance, and aesthetic appeal.
  • The budget for the gate installation and any ongoing maintenance costs should be considered. The cost will vary depending on the type, material, and additional features of the gate.

All About Hammond, LA

About Hammond, LA
  • Hammond is the largest city in Tangipahoa Parish, Louisiana, United States, located 45 miles (72 km) east of Baton Rouge and 45 miles (72 km) northwest of New Orleans.
  • Hammond is home to Southeastern Louisiana University. 
  • It is the principal city of the Hammond metropolitan statistical area, which includes all of Tangipahoa Parish and is a part of the New Orleans-Metairie-Hammond combined statistical area.
Top Attractions
  • Kliebert & Son’s Gator Tours
  • Zemurray Park
  • Tangipahoa African American Heritage Museum
  • Joyce Wildlife Swamp Walk Nature Trail
  • Downtown Hammond
Notable Residents
  • Robert Alford , cornerback for the Arizona Cardinals
  • Kayla Ard , Head Coach for Utah State University Women’s Basketball
  • George W. Bond , fourth acting president of Southeastern Louisiana University (1944–1945); former president of Louisiana Tech University (1928–1936)
  • George S. Bowman Jr. , Major general, U.S. Marine Corps and veteran of World War II, Korea and Vietnam
  • Josh Brooks, athletic director for the University of Georgia
Interesting/Fun Facts
  • The city is named for Peter Hammond (1798–1870) — the surname anglicized from Peter av Hammerdal (Peter of Hammerdal) — a Swedish immigrant who first settled the area around 1818. 
  • Southeastern Louisiana University (SLU), based in Hammond, is one of the state’s regional universities and one of the city’s largest employers.
Public Transportation

Hammond has railways, highways (including the intersection of two interstates), and air travel/transport.
