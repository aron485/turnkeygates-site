Gate Types We Offer

We are a leading provider of top-quality gates for both residential and commercial properties. Access Gates

Access gates are designed to provide controlled entry and exit to a property. These gates offer enhanced security by allowing authorized personnel or vehicles to enter while restricting access to unauthorized individuals. Access gates can be customized with various features such as keypad entry systems, card readers, intercom systems, and even biometric scanners for added security.

Electric Gates

Electric gates are a popular choice for both residential and commercial properties. These gates are automated and can be operated remotely with the help of an electric gate opener. Electric gates not only provide convenience but also enhance security by allowing property owners to control access to their premises. They can be operated using a variety of methods, including keypads, remote controls, or even smartphone apps.

Single Gates

Single gates, also known as pedestrian gates, are designed specifically for individuals on foot. These gates are ideal for properties that have separate entry points for pedestrians and vehicles. Single gates can be customized to match the overall design of the property while providing a secure and aesthetically pleasing access point for pedestrians.

Sliding Gates

Sliding gates are an excellent choice for properties with limited space. These gates operate horizontally, sliding open and closed along a track. Sliding gates are known for their durability, security, and ease of operation. They are an ideal option for properties with a steep driveway or limited space for a swinging gate.

Swing Gates

Swing gates are the classic choice for both residential and commercial properties. These gates operate by swinging open and closed, similar to a door. Swing gates are available in various styles, including single swing gates and double swing gates. They offer a traditional and elegant look while ensuring security and convenience.

Factors Influencing Gate Choices

  • Location: The location of the property plays a crucial role in determining the gate choices. Factors such as the level of security required in the area, the proximity to high-traffic areas, and the overall aesthetic appeal of the gate are considered based on the location.
  • Security requirements: The level of security needed for the property is a significant factor in gate choices. Factors such as the presence of valuable assets, the crime rate in the area, and the desired level of privacy all influence the type of gate chosen.
  • Budget: The available budget for the gate installation and maintenance is an important factor. Different materials, designs, and technologies have varying costs, so the budget will often dictate the gate choices for residential and commercial properties.
  • Purpose and functionality: The purpose and functionality of the gate also influence the choices. Factors such as the intended use (e.g., pedestrian access, vehicle access, or both), the frequency of use, and the desired level of automation or access control will all impact the gate selection.

Choosing the Right Gate Material

Our company in Baton Rouge, LA offers a wide range of gate materials to suit the diverse needs and preferences of their customers. Whether you are looking for a gate that provides security, enhances the aesthetic appeal of your property, or both, we have the perfect solution for you.

Let’s explore the different gate materials we offer and their unique benefits:

1. Aluminum Gates

Aluminum gates are a popular choice for many homeowners due to their durability, low maintenance requirements, and versatility. We offer a variety of aluminum gate designs that can be customized to match your specific style and preferences.

These gates are resistant to rust and corrosion, making them ideal for coastal areas. They can be powder-coated in a wide range of colors to complement the overall look of your property.

2. Steel Gates

If you prioritize security and strength, steel gates are an excellent option. We provide high-quality steel gates that are built to withstand harsh weather conditions and provide maximum security.

These gates can be customized with ornamental designs to add an elegant touch to your property. With proper maintenance, steel gates can last for many years, providing you with peace of mind and protection.

3. Wood Gates

For a timeless and classic look, wood gates are a perfect choice. We offer a selection of wooden gate designs using premium quality wood that is resistant to warping and rot. Our skilled craftsmen can create custom designs that match your unique style and enhance the overall aesthetic of your property. Wood gates provide a warm and welcoming feel, making them a popular choice for residential properties.

4. Vinyl Gates

Vinyl gates are an excellent alternative to wood gates as they offer similar aesthetics but require minimal maintenance. We offer vinyl gates that are resistant to fading, cracking, and peeling, ensuring long-lasting beauty and durability. These gates are available in various colors and styles, allowing you to achieve the desired look for your property without the hassle of frequent upkeep.

5. Wrought Iron Gates

For a sophisticated and elegant appearance, wrought iron gates are a top choice. We provide stunning wrought iron gate designs that exude luxury and enhance the overall curb appeal of your property.

These gates are known for their strength and durability, providing excellent security. Customizable features such as ornamental designs and decorative accents make wrought iron gates a timeless and prestigious option.
